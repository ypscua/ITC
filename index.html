01 / GET TO KNOW ME

About Me.

A little bit about my journey

Hi! I'm Ysabelle Mae Pascua, but you can call me Ysa. I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Ateneo de Davao University.

My college journey didn't exactly follow a straight path. I originally pursued Architecture before deciding to shift to Information Technology. Although I would have been in my second year, changing programs meant beginning a new chapter in a completely different field.

It was a big transition, but I've always been interested in computers, technology, and learning how to write code. I'm excited to explore how programming works and how technology can turn creative ideas into real applications.

Outside academics, music has always been one of the biggest parts of who I am. I love singing, songwriting, playing instruments, and producing my own songs using software such as Ableton Live and Logic Pro.

I hope to release my own music soon and share the stories I've been creating through my songs. For me, both music and programming are forms of creation. One allows me to express emotions, while the other challenges me to think logically and build something from scratch.

Through this journey, I hope to continue growing as both a student and an artist, learning new skills, embracing unfamiliar opportunities, and finding ways to bring my passions together.
